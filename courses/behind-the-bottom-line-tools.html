Prime Cost % = (COGS + Labor) ÷ Revenue × 100. Rows above your target range are flagged in clay; rows below it (which usually means you're running efficiently, or possibly understaffed) are flagged in teal.

Volume effect = (current covers − prior covers) × prior average check. Rate effect = current covers × (current average check − prior average check). The two effects always add up to the total change in revenue.

EBITDA = Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation & Amortization — the operating profit left if you strip out how the company is financed (interest), taxed, and how its assets are expensed over time (D&A). This tool gets there by starting at Net Income and adding those items back, which is mathematically identical to stopping the ladder before they were ever subtracted. This is the “Secret Link” from Module 3: net income doesn't disappear at the bottom of the income statement — it becomes part of Equity on the balance sheet as retained earnings.
