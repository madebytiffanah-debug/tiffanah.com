Try a sample question

One question from the course quiz — see how it feels before you dive in.

A dish costs $3.40 to make and sells for $12.00. A different dish costs $9.80 to make and sells for $24.00. Which one contributes more actual cash profit per plate?

The second dish wins on contribution margin ($24.00 − $9.80 = $14.20) even though its food cost percentage looks worse than the first dish's ($3.40 / $12.00 = 28% vs. $9.80 / $24.00 = 41%). Percentage and dollars can point in opposite directions — that's exactly the trap Module 2 covers.
